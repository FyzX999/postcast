Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Postcast ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Username and password for authentication
  • Content: Videos, titles, descriptions, and metadata you upload
  • Settings: Your preferences and default configurations

2.2 Information from Third-Party Platforms

  • OAuth Tokens: Access tokens to post on your behalf to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, etc.
  • Profile Information: Basic profile data from connected accounts (username, avatar)

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Features used, posts created, timestamps
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Process and publish your video content to selected platforms
  • Generate AI-powered titles and hashtags using Gemini API
  • Store your posts and settings in our database
  • Authenticate and authorize your account
  • Improve and optimize the Service

4. Data Storage

We use the following services to store and process your data:

  • Supabase: Database for posts, settings, and connected accounts
  • Vercel: Application hosting and deployment
  • Google Gemini API: AI content generation (titles, hashtags)

5. Third-Party Services

When you connect your social media accounts, we access them through official APIs:

  • YouTube (Google): YouTube Data API v3
  • TikTok: TikTok Content Posting API
  • Instagram (Meta): Instagram Graph API

Each platform has its own privacy policy and terms of service that apply to your use.

6. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

  • Encrypted authentication tokens using HMAC-SHA256
  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Environment variable protection for API keys
  • OAuth 2.0 for social media authorization

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You can delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data
  • Disconnect social media accounts
  • Opt-out of certain data collection

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use browser localStorage to store authentication tokens for keeping you logged in. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Postcast is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights, contact us at:

Email: privacy@postcast.app

14. Compliance

We comply with applicable data protection laws including GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and other regional privacy regulations.
